# Steps for a clip of the red disc in front of skin (tools/record_clip.py --steps).
# Each line: second of the recording at which the step starts, then what to do.
0   Hold the disc still inside the green box, in front of your neck.
4   Move the disc slowly: left, right, closer to the camera, farther away. Keep it in the picture.
9   Cover the disc completely with your other hand. Keep still.
12  Keep it covered and move hand and disc slowly sideways together.
15  Take the hand away: the disc is visible again. Hold still.
17  Hide the disc behind your back.
19  No disc: neck and face in the picture. Lean closer to the camera, turn your head slowly.
26  Hold your open hand inside the green box, no disc.
28  Bring the disc back and hold it still in front of your neck.
31  Move the disc slowly in front of your neck and face.
35  Done: hold still until the picture says saved.
